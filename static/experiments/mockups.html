Workflow mockups

Four ways to do the same jobs, with made-up plants. Tap one, try it, then come back with ‹. Nothing is saved.

A · Daily wateringWalk roundOne plant at a time, full-screen photo, in room order. Big buttons or swipe: right for Watered, left for Still damp. Built for carrying the phone round the house. B · Daily wateringWater all, then exceptionsThe list, but one tap waters a whole room. Tap any plant that was still damp to flip it. Then Save. Fewest taps when most plants need water. C · Daily wateringPhoto gridBig photo tiles, recognise plants at a glance. Tap a tile for Watered; the small corner button for Still damp. Filter by room. D · SetupCamera-first addingThe camera stays open. Snap, it saves with the room you’re in, snap the next. Room and how often sit under the shutter. Name and interval are optional.

Things to notice: which one you’d actually use on a busy morning, whether you can tell plants apart, and how a mis-tap feels.
