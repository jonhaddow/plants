# Decisions

- **Setup is one photo and a room per plant** (October 2026): the reason there's no app in use is the time other apps take to set up. No species needed: every plant starts weekly (or a rough "every few days / fortnightly / monthly") and learns from there. After saving, the form stays open with the room still picked, for walking round the house.
- **The interval learns from two buttons**, not a care database. Still damp: the interval grows to at least how long it's been plus a bit, and the plant comes back in a quarter of the interval (1 to 7 days). Watered before it was due: the interval moves halfway to the actual gap. Watered late teaches nothing (a busy week, not the plant). 1 to 60 days.
- **No AI in the app** (October 2026): no paid API calls. Identifying species is a batch job: Claude Code (Jon's subscription) looks at the photos and fills in names, `species`, `notes` and starting intervals (the `classify-plants` repo skill). New plants wait under **To set up** on the Plants page (`needs_setup`) until it runs; ones it can't name confidently are called `[unknown]`, with suggestions in the note, rather than guessed. Pl@ntNet's free API is the option if it's ever wanted in the app.
- **The same kind in the same room is one tile** (October 2026): with 56 plants, Today showed about 25 on one day, mostly sets of spider plants, peace lilies and so on that get watered together. Today and the morning push stack plants with the same `species` in the same room ("Spider plant ×3"): one tap waters the stack, and its ×3 splits it up when one is still damp. Each plant still keeps its own interval, so if they drift apart they stop being due on the same day. Only the display changes, not the data. Turned down: merging them into one plant with a count (they'd lose their own photos and intervals) and groups you set up yourself (more to set up). Plants without a species (`[unknown]`) are never stacked.
- **A watering can be put in late** (October 2026): a plant's page has "Watered on a different day?", a date up to 30 days back. It's an ordinary Watered check on that day, so the due date counts from it, it shows in History (which goes by day) and Undo works. Turned down: editing the last-watered date as a plain field, which would skip History and the learning.
- **Undo after every tap**: each check stores the schedule before it, so a mis-tap on a phone puts the plant back exactly.
- **Photos shrunk on the phone** (1200px JPEG, about 200 KB) before upload, and kept in D1 as base64 (`photo` table), served only to signed-in people, with a new key per photo so they cache for good. Not R2: it needs turning on with a card, and the Cloudflare token has no R2 access. A house of plants is a few tens of MB against D1's 5 GB. Move to R2 if photos ever get big or many.
- **One morning push to everyone**, about 8am UK (7:07 UTC cron: 8:07 in summer, 7:07 in winter), naming plants room by room. Nothing if nothing's due. Shared, not per person: either of you can do the watering.
- **Its own look**: Nunito, sage and cream, leaf green for actions and a water blue for Watered. Budget's floating tab bar.
- **Follows the device's light or dark setting**; no theme setting.
- **Rooms are made while adding plants**; no rooms page yet (see roadmap).
- **Experiments are public static files** (October 2026): clickable mockups in `static/experiments`, linked from Settings, with made-up plants. Static assets skip the sign-in check, which is fine with no real data in them.
- **Photo grid for Today, camera-first for adding** (October 2026), picked from the workflow mockups. Today becomes big photo tiles: plants are known by sight, not name, so tap a tile for Watered and a small corner button for Still damp. Adding keeps the camera open and saves on each snap, with the room under the shutter, to make walking round the house even quicker. Turned down: walk round (one plant at a time, swipe; slow with many plants) and water all then exceptions (a Save step, and easy to water a whole room by mistake). Built October 2026; the mockups page is gone.
- **Checked plants stay on Today** until tomorrow, tinted, and a second tap undoes them: both of you can see what's done, and tiles never move under a thumb (they're in name order within a room, not most overdue first; a "late" badge shows that instead).
- **Coming up is a row per day** (October 2026), with a count and a few photos, tapped open to a small grid: with most plants due within a week, a row per plant ran to dozens.
- **The camera page uses the camera in the page** (`getUserMedia`, so `Permissions-Policy` allows `camera=(self)`). Without one, or without permission, the shutter opens the phone's own camera each time; Photos picks from the library. Each photo saves straight away, with Undo, rather than a form to submit.
