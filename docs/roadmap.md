# Roadmap

## Live

- Today as a photo grid: tap a tile for Watered, the corner button for Still damp, tap again to undo; filter by room; the week ahead
- Camera-first adding: the camera stays open and each photo saves a plant to the room picked under the shutter; name and how often behind Details
- Plants by room; edit; delete; history
- Morning push and app icon badge

## Next

- Batch species ID: photos to Claude Code, which fills in species, notes and better starting intervals
- Rooms page: rename, reorder (the walking route), delete empty ones
- Holiday mode: a page for a plant-sitter, signed in by link
- Winter: stretch intervals in the darker months
- QR / NFC tags on pots that open the plant's page
